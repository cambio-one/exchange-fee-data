# eth-arbitrum → eth-ethereum, 2026-10-12 to 2026-10-18 (UTC)

- `website-checks.csv`: 168 checks of ChangeNOW, SimpleSwap, each on the exchange's own public website: what it would deliver for the amount sent, beside the market rate at that moment.
- `cambio-quotes.csv`: 8 Cambio quotes on the same route in the same week, with the network fee Cambio stated.

The exchanges in this folder are the ones we checked, not every exchange. Published once and never edited; a correction is a new folder.
